The Founding Story

It started in a
high-desert garage.

In 2008, two working guides — Maya and Jasper — pooled their savings, bought a retired forest-service 4×4 and ran their first four-day traverse for six strangers who'd found them on a hiking forum.

Nobody in that first group had ever been guided before. By the summit everyone had. The rule they wrote that week still governs every trip today: the terrain decides the route, the guide decides the pace, and no traveler is ever left to feel like cargo.

Eighteen seasons later the truck is gone, the group is bigger, but the departure list still reads like the one pinned to that garage wall.

What We Won't Compromise

The ground rules of the house

Safety, non-negotiable

Every guide is a certified wilderness first responder. Gear is inspected before and after every departure, and every route has a written bail plan.

Eight travelers, max

A hard cap on group size, not a marketing line. Eight means one guide ratio that lets everyone actually hear the mountains — and each other.

Leave it better

We follow Leave No Trace to the letter and run one trail-maintenance weekend per season for every route in the catalog. The ground we guide pays us back.
