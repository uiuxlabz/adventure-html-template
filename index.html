Who We Are

Built by guides,
not by a sales floor.

Adventure started in 2008 as two guides and one beat-up 4×4 hauling hikers into the high desert. Eighteen seasons later we still run every trip ourselves — same crews, same peaks, same rule: the terrain decides the day, and our people come home safe.

No bus tours, no assembly lines. Every expedition on this site is scouted on foot, capped at eight travelers, and led by a guide who has crossed that ground more times than they can count.

What We Do

Six ways to leave the trailhead

One crew, one standard. Pick a discipline — every route below runs with the same small-group, guide-led setup.

Guided Hiking

Day hikes and multi-day treks on routes we maintain ourselves — alpine passes, canyon floors and ridgelines with no trailhead crowds.

Backcountry Camping

Leave-the-tent-behind style — lightweight base camps, fire-cooked meals and nights under skies you forgot existed.

River & Kayak

Guided river days for every level — glassy lake paddles up to class III rapids with the boats, gear and safety crew provided.

Rock & Ice Climb

Top-roped instruction to multi-pitch leads. We bolt, clean and rig every route — you bring the nerve, we bring everything else.

Wildlife Safaris

Morning and dusk game drives with a naturalist guide — bighorn, pronghorn, eagles and the occasional gray wolf, at a respectful distance.

Custom Expeditions

Private groups, corporate retreats and once-in-a-lifetime routes. Tell us the map coordinates — we'll build the whole trip around them.
